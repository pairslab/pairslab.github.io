# Synthetic research-media drafts

This directory preserves historical AI-generated motion studies and poster frames for internal review only.

- Hugo does not publish `working-assets/`.
- Do not reference these files from `data/research.yaml` or `data/research_media.yaml`.
- Public research media must come from an identifiable PAIRS Lab paper, project, experiment, or approved real-world demonstration.
- New synthetic drafts generated by `scripts/generate-research-editorial-videos.mjs` are written to `generated/`.

The files remain here to retain design history; they are not evidence of a deployed research system.
