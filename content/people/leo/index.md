Leo is currently pursuing a PhD degree at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). Since August 2026, he has been a Visiting PhD Student at PAIRS Lab, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). His research interests include autonomous agent systems, world models for embodied intelligence, long-term robot memory, and embodied intelligence. He is particularly interested in developing embodied agents that can learn, adapt, and reason over extended interactions.
