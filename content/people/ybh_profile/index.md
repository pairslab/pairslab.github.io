Bohan You obtained his MSc in Computer Control & Automation from Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. He is now working as a Research Assistant at PAIRS Lab, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). His current research focuses on robot navigation, robust SLAM, multi-sensor fusion, and degeneration-aware perception.
