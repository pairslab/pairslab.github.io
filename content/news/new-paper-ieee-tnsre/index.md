Fangqiang Ding and collaborators published **“AI-based Digital Assessment of Gross Motor Function in Metachromatic Leukodystrophy”** in *IEEE Transactions on Neural Systems and Rehabilitation Engineering*.

The work studies an AI-based approach to digitally assessing gross motor function in metachromatic leukodystrophy.
