Two PAIRS Lab teams were recognized in the creative competition at the 2026 Safety and Emergency Rescue Robotics Competition (Guangzhou).

- **PAIRS-Rescue** received Second Prize.
- **PAIRS-Pi** placed ninth.

The competition provided an early opportunity for the lab to develop and test ideas in robot perception, decision-making, autonomous action, and task execution for challenging rescue environments.
