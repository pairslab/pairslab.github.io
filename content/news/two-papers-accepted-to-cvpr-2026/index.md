Two papers co-authored by PAIRS Lab PI Fangqiang Ding have been accepted to [CVPR 2026](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026):

- [**M4Human**](https://fanjunqiao.github.io/M4Human-site/): *A Large-Scale Multimodal mmWave Radar Benchmark for Human Mesh Reconstruction*
- [**PALM**](https://plan-lab.github.io/palm): *Progress-Aware Policy Learning via Affordance Reasoning for Long-Horizon Robotic Manipulation*
