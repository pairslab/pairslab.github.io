Fangqiang Ding joined the Robotics and Autonomous Systems Thrust at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) as a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor and founded the Physical AI and Robotic Systems Lab.

PAIRS Lab develops trustworthy and scalable Physical AI for human–machine symbiosis.
