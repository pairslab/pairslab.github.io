Physical AI

Physical AI brings intelligence into systems that sense, learn, reason, and act in the physical world. It connects perception with real-world dynamics, uncertainty, contact, and human behavior so that decisions lead to safe and dependable action.

PAIRS Lab studies the sensing, learning, and control foundations needed for trustworthy and scalable Physical AI across mobile robots, human-centered systems, manipulation, and data-driven robot learning.
